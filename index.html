01
Self-initiated demo · Working demo↗

Luxe Vita Store

A wholesale product catalogue concept for a small business in Osogbo, Osun State. Customers can browse products, choose quantities, and prepare one complete enquiry through WhatsApp.

The challenge

Wholesale buyers often negotiate orders through WhatsApp, but product details and quantities can become scattered across several messages.

What I built

A catalogue that lets customers collect products and quantities across different categories before sending the complete request to the seller in one prepared WhatsApp message.

Why it helps

The seller receives a clearer enquiry from the start, while customers get an easier way to organise what they want to buy.

02
Personal build↗

Drayline Logistics

A concept website for a freight and customs brokerage. Instead of looking like a static brochure, it gives visitors a quick feel for the kind of information they need when planning and following a shipment.

The challenge

Many logistics websites simply list their services. I wanted this one to feel useful as soon as it opens, with information that looks and behaves like part of an active operation.

What I built

A live-style departure board, a shipment-tracking experience, and a simple rate estimator that help visitors explore the service before making an enquiry.

Why it helps

The website gives potential customers more reasons to interact, understand the service, and take the next step.

03
Personal build↗

Mollusca

A food-scanning concept that turns a health score into a practical next step: a homemade recipe matched to the food being explored.

The challenge

A score on its own is not always useful. People are more likely to act when an app helps them understand what they can do next.

What I built

A friendly scanning experience that presents a result, explains it simply, and pairs it with a recipe that someone could make at home.

Why it helps

The concept connects information with action, making the experience more useful than a number alone.
